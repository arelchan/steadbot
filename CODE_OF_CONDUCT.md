# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a straightforward experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Things that keep this project worth working on: being direct without being unkind, accepting that other
people have context you do not, disagreeing about the work rather than about the person, and saying
plainly when you were wrong.

Things that are not acceptable: sexualised language or imagery, trolling, insults, personal or political
attacks, harassment in public or private, publishing others' private information without permission, and
any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when representing the project
elsewhere.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **conduct@evermind.ai**. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers
will remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban a contributor
temporarily or permanently for behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening or harmful.
